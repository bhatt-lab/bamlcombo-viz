Combination Index (CI) Interpretation

CI (HSA) = DSS(Best monotherapy) / DSS(Combination)

  • CI > 1: Combination is not better than its monotherapy
  • CI = 1: Combination is as effective as its best constituent monotherapy
  • CI < 1: Combination is more effective than its monotherapy
