Combination Ratio (CR) Interpretation

CR (HSA) = DSS(Best monotherapy) / DSS(Combination)

  • CR > 1: Combination is not better than its monotherapy
  • CR = 1: Combination is as effective as its best constituent monotherapy
  • CR < 1: Combination is more effective than its monotherapy

Dataset Documentation

Overview

This dashboard provides access to the Beat AML drug combination dataset, integrating high-throughput ex vivo drug response data with clinical and molecular metadata. The goal is to enable exploration of treatment response patterns and biomarker associations in acute myeloid leukemia (AML).

How to Use This Portal

Summary

Overview of cohort characteristics.

Clinical Data

Interactive exploration of sample-level clinical and molecular metadata.

Drug Response

Visualization of monotherapy and combination sensitivity profiles.

Custom Plots

User-defined analyses integrating drug response, mutations, transcriptomics, proteomics, and clinical variables.

Study Design

  • Cohort: 596 AML samples from 474 patients in the Beat AML study.
  • Dataset: Ex vivo drug sensitivity screening.
  • Assay: Freshly isolated MNCs from primary patient bone marrow, peripheral blood, or leukapheresis specimens were seeded into prepared drug plates at 1E4 cells per well. Inhibitors (single agents or equimolar combinations of two single agents) were evaluated in a concentration series ranging from 0.0137 μM to 10 μM (except for dasatinib and sunitinib; 0.00137 μM to 1 μM). Cell viability was assessed after 3 days using an MTS reagent (CellTiter96; Promega).
  • Drug Sensitivity Score (DSS): Calculated using DSS2 as described in Yadav et al., 2014. The calculated DSS values were standardized to a range of 0 to 1, with values closer to 1 indicating a stronger sensitivity to the drug. Replicates for each sample are averaged.
  • Metadata: Clinical, mutation, RNA-seq, proteomics.
  • Data Sources: Patient sample clinical summary, RNA-sequencing data, WES/targeted sequencing mutations calls and raw inhibitor values from the Beat AML cohort are downloaded from the publicly accessible interactive user interface, Vizome and their Github page. Processed Beat AML global proteomics data are accessed via the Synapse repository from the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory - Proteogenomic Translational Research Center.

Data Files

Clinical: Patient demographics and clinical annotations
Mutation: Gene mutation status from targeted and whole-exome sequencing (Converted to 0/1)
RNA Sequencing: Gene expression (VST normalized counts)
Proteomics: Protein abundance measurements (log₂-transformed and zero-centered)
DSS-Monotherapy: Drug sensitivity scores for single agents
DSS-Combination: Drug sensitivity scores for combination therapies
